# Sound sources

All sounds are from the original Mortal Kombat (1992) arcade game. Mortal
Kombat, its sounds and music are the property of their respective owners
(see the disclaimer in the README).

## Music

From the arcade gamerip on KHInsider
(https://downloads.khinsider.com/, album "Mortal Kombat (Arcade Gamerip)
(1992)", music by Dan Forden), added by the user on 2026-09-24.

| File | Track | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `character-select-theme.mp3` | 03. Battle Plan | Character select, looped from inside (the rip fades out; see `CS_THEME_LOOP_START` in `src/audio.rs`) |
| `test-your-might-theme.mp3` | 14. Test Your Might | Start of every round, once |
